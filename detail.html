Additional Information about me

I have been interested in computers since lower secondary school, beginning with learning fundamental skills such as computer assembly, operating system installation, and basic troubleshooting. This curiosity motivated me to explore the underlying mechanisms of technology more deeply.

In upper secondary school, I further developed my programming abilities, starting with Python and expanding to other languages such as HTML, CSS, Java, Golang, React, Flutter, and C. I also gained hands-on experience in developing websites and applications. In addition, I learned about hardware through working with Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and Micro:bit. I was also introduced to networking concepts by my father, which strengthened my understanding of system connectivity from a broader perspective.

With the foundation and experience I have built, I am determined to pursue my studies at King Mongkut’s Institute of Technology Ladkrabang to enhance my capabilities and apply my knowledge to create innovative solutions that contribute sustainably to society.
